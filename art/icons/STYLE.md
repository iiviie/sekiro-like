# LAST BLADE icon style bible

Paste this before every image prompt so every icon reads as one set.

STYLE: bold stylised game icon, Japanese ink-brush (sumi-e) meets clean cel-shaded mobile-game icon.
Thick confident black brush outline (about 4% of the image width) around the whole silhouette, with
slightly rough dry-brush edges. Flat cel shading with exactly two tones per material plus one crisp
highlight. Subtle washi-paper grain inside the fills only. Chunky, readable silhouette that still reads
at 64x64 pixels. Three-quarter view from slightly above, single object, centred, filling ~80% of the frame.
PALETTE (hex): crimson #C8102E, deep lacquer red #7A0A1A, bone white #EDE3CF, gold leaf #D4A54A,
dark bronze #6B4A22, charcoal #1A1718, jade #3E9E84, steel #B8C2CC.
LIGHT: warm key light from the top left, cool shadow.
BACKGROUND: perfectly flat, pure white (#FFFFFF), edge to edge, no shadow, no vignette, no ground plane.
NEVER: text, letters, numbers, watermarks, logos, frames or borders, photorealism, gradients behind the
object, multiple objects unless asked.
NO BLOOD OR GORE, EVER (Roblox moderates uploads for gore): no blood, no red liquid of any kind, no red splatter,
splashes, drips, droplets, pools or stains, no wounds, cuts in flesh or bodies, no blade stabbing into anything red.
Crimson appears only as lacquer, cloth, a flat seal or UI colour, or clearly dry ink brushwork (no wet drips).
Show impact and finisher energy with black ink brush arcs, white slash light, gold sparks, petals, feathers or lightning.
